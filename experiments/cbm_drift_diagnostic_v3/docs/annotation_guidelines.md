# Deferred Real Annotation

Two annotators independently examine task requirements, formal evidence through
the target stage, the agent's legal visible history and the target text. The
page excludes Judge predictions and program semantic/time classifications.
Current absent target texts are explicitly not_generated, not synthetic.

Mark current_deviation yes/no/uncertain, answer/content distinctions, exact claim
quote and stance, candidate/attribute/rule objects, evidence versions, necessary
prior own judgments, temporal status and uncertainty. A correct answer may have
false reasoning. No relevant explanation may leave F/R/I unresolved.

Use annotation_status not_annotated, uncertain, annotated or adjudicated.
An annotated explicit negative must say current_deviation=no. Empty unannotated
claims are not negatives. Independent agreement is followed by adjudication;
agreement between two Judge modes is not truth. Do not claim hidden cognition.

The132 targets are selected without reading correctness or predicted labels.
Shared T1-T3 targets occur once; T4/T5 targets use paired agent/round choices
across four branches. Missing targets stay missing rather than being replaced
by convenient successful messages. Real264 Judge calls remain deferred.
